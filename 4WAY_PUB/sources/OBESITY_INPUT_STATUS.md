# Obesity input — primary source opened, figures still at one remove

**Primary identified and opened:** NCD Risk Factor Collaboration. *Obesity rise
plateaus in developed nations and accelerates in developing nations.* **Nature**
2026;653(8114):510-518. DOI 10.1038/s41586-026-10383-0. Covers **1980-2024**.

**The figures used in this kit are not in it as stated.** The 6.8% (1980) ->
17.2% (2024) women's obesity series came from the World Obesity Federation
reporting NCD-RisC, not from NCD-RisC's own paper, which presents results by
country grouping rather than as a consolidated global figure. What the Nature
paper does state: "from 1980 to 2024, age-standardized prevalence of obesity in
women increased with a PP > 0.80 in 183 countries," with increases ranging "from
2 to 43 percentage points for women."

**Status: partially closed.** The source is identified and the year range
matches. The specific percentages remain at one remove and should be taken from
the NCD-RisC downloadable dataset before they are relied upon.

Everything computed from those percentages — the all / half / none obesity
scaling across the 1950-2024 series — inherits that.
