## Title

    An Undeclared Measurement Interval: signal averaging time in

Titled to the method. Not a death count. No person named.

## Description — falsifiers first

    WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH
    - No trial shows that screening pregnant women for sleep-disordered
      breathing improves outcomes. None exists. That absence is the USPSTF's
      own stated reason for its Grade I rating.
    - The central prospective study on fetal growth (Fung 2013) has n=41;
      adjusted for BMI its association weakens to p=0.06.
    - Most of this literature measures apnea-hypopnea indices, not oxygen
      desaturation. Arguing from it to "measure oxygen at night" is an
      inference, and is labelled as one throughout.
    - Nothing here identifies any person's intent. The absence of a global
      prevalence figure for mouth breathing is documented. Why it is absent
      is not.
    - The 1-in-12-million figure in the correction log is e^(-np) under an
      independence assumption that is false. Research topics are chosen by
      funding, billing and category. It is not a posterior on anything that
      occurred, and is published only to show why the coincidence framing
      fails.

    WHAT IT CONTAINS
    Three findings, each with its own limits stated: that a portable
    non-invasive blood-oxygen instrument has existed since 1940; that maternal
    nocturnal desaturation is associated with impaired fetal growth; and that
    no body recommends screening pregnant women for it, with the USPSTF
    excluding pregnant persons from the question entirely.

    A correction log of eleven numbered errors made during assembly, each with
    the defect named. Runnable code reproducing every figure, with sourced and
    estimated inputs kept in separate files. A source index recording what was
    opened and what was paywalled or blocked.

    Medicare requires a 4% oxygen desaturation to score a hypopnea; the AASM
    uses 3%. Under 4%, 48.9% of women would be undiagnosed against 28.5% of
    men. Who set 4%, and on what evidence, is posed here as an open question
    with a documentary trail. It is not answered and no one is accused.
