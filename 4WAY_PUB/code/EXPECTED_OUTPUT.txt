WHAT IS COUNTED, AND WHAT IS NOT
==========================================================
  hypertension, adults 30-79 ......           1.4 B   WHO 2025
  sleep apnea, adults 30-69 .......           0.9 B   Benjafield 2019
  mouth breathing, any age ........ NO FIGURE EXISTS

  Mouth breathing is measured at 26-56% in Brazilian schoolchildren
  (Martins 2014, n=419, ages 6-11: 56.8%). There is no adult
  prevalence figure, in any country. A condition running at that rate
  in children cannot plausibly be rarer in adults than a condition
  affecting 1.4 billion people. The question has not been asked.
