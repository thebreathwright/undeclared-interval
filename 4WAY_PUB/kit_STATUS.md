# r2 Source-Kit Status — Historical

The next sections report the r2 source state. They are not the current
publication state: GitHub is public and retrieval-verified; the other three
ways remain open as recorded in `RELEASE_CLOSURE_RECORD.v1.md`.

**It is not a measurement.** It is a machine with inputs. Change the inputs and
every number changes. That is the design, not a defect — but a default printed
by a script is an argument, not a finding. See `corrections/UNAUDITED.md`.

**Several inputs are at one remove.** Births reached this desk through a
rate-table rather than UN WPP opened directly. Obesity reached it through the
World Obesity Federation reporting NCD-RisC, not NCD-RisC. Won et al. reached it
through a scoping review. Each is flagged where it appears; none has been read at
source.

## The asymmetry that matters more than anything else in this folder

On **14 September 2025** a mortality model was sent to 66 institutional
addresses, with eight payload digests and a server-assigned Message-ID. Whatever
its merits, that object is **dated, distributed, and has priority**. It is the
public artifact.

The corrected work — the one with the falsifiers, the correction log, the
separated sourced and estimated inputs — **has no public identifier at all.**

So the position today is: the uncorrected instrument is the one the world can
find, and the corrected one is on a laptop. A deposit does not retract the 2025
model, does not notify anyone who received it, and does not sit anywhere those
recipients could encounter it.

**That gap is not closed by anything in this folder.** It is closed by a deposit
that is actually made, and by whatever the author decides to say about the
earlier object. Neither is done.

## Known artifacts of construction

**The three prevalence models converge in 2024 by construction.** Obesity is
scaled to a 2024 anchor, so all / half / none collide at the right edge. The
chart therefore *looks like* "today we finally know." It is an artifact of the
chosen axis, not a narrowing of uncertainty. Today's real uncertainty — 15% to
25% prevalence — is the widest band in the series, not the narrowest.

**The 1-in-12-million figure was rendered at headline size.** It is e^(-np) under
an independence assumption that is false. It is not the odds of anything that
occurred. Setting it in large type after a request to be impressed is precisely
the move that gets a document dismissed at intake, and it was made here.
