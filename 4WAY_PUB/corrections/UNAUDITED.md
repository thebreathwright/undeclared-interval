# Unaudited inputs — do not screenshot these as findings

## n = 163,000 physicians

Not a sourced headcount. Assembled as: nine institutions drawn from a BRIMR
top-20 NIH-funded cohort recorded in a prior session, times a medical-school
class-size ramp of 50 (1861) to 175 (2026) invented by this assembler. No
registrar data, no alumni records, no BRIMR audit was performed.

Any figure computed from it — the 1.28M and 14.1M person-year estimates, and the
1-in-12-million coincidence calculation — inherits that. **Change n and the
output changes. That is what the machine is for.**

## The 1-in-12-million figure specifically

It is e^(-np) under an independence assumption that is **false**. Research
topics are chosen by funding, billing category and publishability, not
independently. The number is not a posterior on steering, on intent, or on
anything that occurred. It is published to show why the coincidence framing
fails, and for no other purpose.

## Every multiplier in code/estimated_series.py

1.50, 1.25, 1.15, 1.12, 1.30, 1.08 — all estimates by this assembler. None
sourced. Direction argued; magnitude asserted.

## Class of error this file guards against

A default value printed by a script and screenshotted becomes a citation. The
defaults here are arguments, not measurements.
